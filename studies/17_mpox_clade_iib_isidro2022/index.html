Biological Narrative

This study presents a complete, deterministic replication and validation of the Mpox Virus Clade IIb molecular dating benchmark originally published by Isidro et al. (Nature Medicine, 2022) and extended by O'Toole et al. (Science, 2023). The analysis evaluates $N = 57$ complete coding sequences (25,359 nucleotide positions in-frame, 8,453 codons) sampled across human clinical cases in Nigeria, Europe, the Americas, and Asia between 2017 and 2025 (temporal timespan: 2017.87 to 2025.83 CE, spanning 8.0 years).
