Biological Narrative

This study presents a complete, deterministic replication and validation of the Canine Parvovirus (CPV) molecular dating benchmark originally published by Shackelton et al. (PNAS, 2005). The analysis evaluates $N = 152$ coding sequences (1,755 nucleotide positions in-frame, 585 codons) of the major capsid protein VP2 gene sampled across domestic dogs between 1997 and 2016 (temporal timespan: 1997.50 to 2016.14 CE, spanning 18.6 years).
