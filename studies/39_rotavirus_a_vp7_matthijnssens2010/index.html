Biological Narrative

Segmented Double-Stranded RNA Reassortment and Multi-Lineage Rate Heterogeneity. Rotaviruses possess an 11-segment double-stranded RNA genome characterized by frequent inter-lineage reassortment and antigenetic shifts. The outer capsid glycoprotein VP7 defines G-serotype specificity. Under published BEAST MCMC with an uncorrelated relaxed clock, the global common ancestor was estimated at 1960.0 CE [1950, 1970 CE].
