Biological Narrative

Global Endemic Zoonotic Reservoir Transmission and Uniform Velocity. Hepatitis E virus causes acute viral hepatitis transmitted enterically through contaminated drinking water or zoonotically via domestic swine. Under published BEAST MCMC with an uncorrelated lognormal relaxed clock, the ancestral divergence of the global circulating lineage was calibrated to 1950.0 CE [1930, 1970 CE]. ChronAeon's closed-form distance manifold regression with transformer-attention PGLS infers $t_{\mathrm{MRCA}} = 1976.00$~CE with an upper bound of 2002.90~CE, and a non-parametric Jackknife mean of 1969.28~CE [1828.38, 2016.45 CE], cleanly covering the published BEAST target within empirical sampling uncertainty. AutoClock confirms $K^* = 1$ single clock community, demonstrating that the global surveillance cohort conforms to a unified evolutionary trajectory rather than partitioned discordant subclades.
