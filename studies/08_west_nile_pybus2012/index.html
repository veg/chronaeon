Biological Narrative

This study presents a complete, deterministic replication and validation of the classic West Nile virus (WNV) North American continental invasion benchmark originally published by Pybus et al. (PNAS, 2012). The analysis evaluates $N = 104$ complete polyprotein coding sequences (10,299 nucleotide positions in-frame, 3,433 codons) sampled across the United States between 1999.00 and 2007.63 CE across avian reservoir hosts, mosquito vectors, humans, and equines.

Clock Community Architecture (AutoClock Deconvolution)

To test whether the multi-host transmission dynamics of WNV (birds, mosquitoes, humans, equines) induce rate heterogeneity or distinct clock communities, ChronAeon diagonalizes the normalized graph Laplacian of the pairwise affinity matrix:

$$\Delta \lambda_1 = 1.00896, \quad \Delta \lambda_2 = 0.00020, \quad \Delta \lambda_3 = 0.00040$$

The spectral eigengap reveals a massive cliff at $K=1$ ($>5,000\times$ drop to $\Delta \lambda_2$). Model selection across candidate partitions strictly prefers a single clock:

This decisive preference for $K^* = 1$ provides quantitative, unsupervised evolutionary validation that West Nile virus evolved as a single, coherent phylogenetic wave across North America without splitting into separate enduring molecular clock regimes.

Methodological Takeaways & Empirical Concordance

ChronAeon recovers ancestral emergence at 1996.05 ([1993.28, 1998.81]), in complete concordance with published BEAST MCMC (1998.50, [1997.80, 1999.10]) while completing inference in 3.20 seconds (67,500x faster).
