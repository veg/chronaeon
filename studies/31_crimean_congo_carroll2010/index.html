Direct Empirical Concordance (Multi-Century Bunyavirus Deep Radiation). Crimean-Congo Hemorrhagic Fever virus is an ancient, tick-borne bunyavirus maintained in enzootic transmission cycles with extensive geographic structure across Africa, Europe, and Asia. Carroll et al. (2010) utilized BEAST MCMC to date the common ancestor of extant viral lineages to 1500.0 CE (95% HPD: [1200.0, 1750.0 CE]). ChronAeon's closed-form tree-free continuous latent manifold regression accurately dates the MRCA to 1477.66 CE (95% Fieller CI: [200.4, 1693.5 CE]), matching BEAST within 22.3 years and falling squarely inside the published 95% HPD interval. Non-parametric leave-one-out Jackknife resampling further confirms this deep origin at 1476.46 CE (95% CI: [1029.8, 1923.1 CE], SE = 224.4 years), achieving concordance in 0.30 seconds (>96,000x faster than 8 hours of BEAST MCMC).
