Biological Narrative

Direct Empirical Concordance. The initial emergence of SARS-CoV-1 in Guangdong province, China occurred in late autumn 2002, prior to international detection in February 2003. Published BEAST 1.10 MCMC under an uncorrelated lognormal relaxed clock estimated an ancestral emergence of 2002.80 CE [2002.50, 2003.00 CE] (mid-October 2002). ChronAeon's closed-form tree-free latent manifold regression infers $t_{\mathrm{MRCA}} = 2002.84$ CE [2002.79, 2002.88 CE] ($\mu = 3.81 \times 10^{-3}$ substitutions per site per year, $R^2 = 0.982$, Fieller $g = 0.0033$), matching BEAST within 0.04 years (14.1 days) in 0.34 seconds (>75,000x faster than MCMC).
