Biological Narrative

This study presents a complete, deterministic replication and validation of the landmark Zika virus (ZIKV) Americas molecular dating benchmark originally published by Faria et al. (Science, 2016). The analysis evaluates $N = 103$ coding sequences (1,512 nucleotide positions in-frame, 504 codons) encoding the polyprotein core, sampled across the Americas, the Caribbean, and ancestral Pacific reference strains between **2013.12 and 2021.71 CE** (temporal timespan: 8.59 years).
