Biological Narrative

This study presents a complete, deterministic replication and validation of the Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) molecular dating benchmark originally published by Zehender et al. (Journal of Virology, 2014). The analysis evaluates $N = 173$ coding sequences (681 nucleotide positions in-frame, 227 codons) of the surface antigen (S gene) sampled across European cohorts between 1997 and 2010 (temporal timespan: 1997.50 to 2010.50 CE, spanning 13.0 years).
